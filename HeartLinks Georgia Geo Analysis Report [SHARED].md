# Overview

# **HeartLinks Geo Analysis Report — Simple Outline \+ How to Read It**

## **What the report is for (1 sentence)**

To show **where closed-won clients actually came from** and whether those locations match **where we’re targeting Google Ads**.

---

# **How the report is laid out (same for every state)**

## Recommendations 

The recommendations on locations to target or discontinue targeting.

## **Section 1\) In-target vs Out-of-target (Counts)**

**What you learn:**  
 Are we winning deals **inside** our targeted areas?

**How to use it:**  
 This is the fastest “is targeting working?” checkpoint.

---

## **Section 2\) In-target win locations (City / State / ZIP)**

**What you learn:**  
 Exactly **which locations are producing closed-won deals** inside targeting.

**How to use it:**  
 These are your **core geos to keep and protect**.

---

##    **Section 3\) Out-of-target win locations (City / State / ZIP)**

**What you learn:**  
 Which closed-won deals came from **outside targeting**.

**How to use it:**  
 These are the “decision geos”:

* Do we **expand** to include them?

* Or do we **tighten** targeting and ignore them?

---

## **Section 4\) Contextual signals (Why these wins happened)**

**What you learn:**  
 What patterns show up in the winning deals, such as:

* Paid Search vs Offline Sources

* CTM (call tracking) signals

* Drilldowns (campaign/keyword intent)

* Message themes (high-level only)

**How to use it:**  
 This explains **why the numbers look the way they do** and what kind of traffic converts best.
